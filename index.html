SERVER STAFF ROLES & DUTIES

Review expectations and rules for all ranks. Inactive or rule-breaking staff are replaced promptly.

// STAFF HIERARCHY

[TESTER]

Tier I: QA Tester

The entry-level testing team. No moderation tools, strictly focused on stress testing and breaking the server.

  • [REWARD] $1,000 in-game money per verified bug reported.
  • [PERKS] Custom debug commands, skins, and experimental items.
  • [STRICT] Giving away exclusive items or abusing bugs = demotion & ban.
[HELPER]

Tier II: Helper

Front-line community defenders. Focuses on chat safety and helping newer players navigate the server.

  • [COMMANDS] /mute, /tempmute, and /kick. (No tempban or permaban).
  • [FOCUS] Stop hate speech, toxicity, and shady outside links.
  • [ESCALATE] Request a Mod via the portal, or work with Testers to report bugs.
[MOD]

Tier III: Moderator

Senior enforcement staff in close direct contact with the owner. Responsible for active security.

  • [COMMANDS] Full server moderation, bans, and enforcement perms.
  • [EVIDENCE] You MUST have a solid reason and proof for every punishment.
  • [STRICT] Power abuse will lead to an instant permanent server ban.

// GUIDELINES & LEAVE POLICY

INACTIVITY & BREAKS

Demotions are almost always caused by silent inactivity. Need a break for exams, studying, or family? Message me beforehand! We will grant excused time off. Unexcused absence = demotion.

PRIVATE BOARD LEAKS

All staff (Mods & Testers) must monitor the private board. Leaking internal messages is strictly forbidden. Doing so will result in an immediate demotion and a permanent IP ban.

APPLYING & RE-APPLYING

Past server/Minecraft experience gives you a boost, but anyone can apply. If a staff member is removed, the next qualified applicant is chosen. Demoted staff are always eligible to re-apply.

Got questions? Contact owner / staff support in #support-ticket
